What's your temperament?

God gave each of us a natural way of being: how we think, feel, and react. The old name for it is temperament. There are four, and none is better than another.

Eight quick questions. Pick the answer that sounds most like you most of the time, not who you wish you were. No names, nothing is saved or sent. It all stays on your phone.
